## Welcome to WeAware.
WeAware aims to provide you with all the perspectives from all sides. We try to find every source for all the happenings in the world.
We cluster simililar news together and maintain a comprehensive collection of articles, dates, and important events.
Using this large database, we,
1. Have an Instagram automation which covers 12 stories a day using the WeAware account. You can find it at: https://www.instagram.com/weawarenews/
2. Post articles to the website and app, the articles are generated through a strict multi-agent pipeline which provides a consensus report after considering perspectives from all the sources. These are written daily about the matters of the previous day.
3. A Newsletter containing the latest and most important articles is e-mailed on schedule to willing subscribers (daily or weekly)
4. A Podcast episode for people on the go, or ones who might just prefer to listen is generated everyday, 2 narrators discuss and cover 3-4 major stories of the day.
5. Sherlock Mode (Experimental) this feature lets users investigate into stories on their own. The "Journalist Jeff" scours the internet and generates a report of any historical/current news story you may be curious about.
6. A native Andriod and Mobile App covering all the features in the website and more...
